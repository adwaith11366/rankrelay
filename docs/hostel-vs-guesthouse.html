Hostel or guesthouse: which is cheaper for solo travellers?

A hostel dorm bed is usually the cheapest bed in town, but a guesthouse room can beat it when two people split the cost.

When a hostel wins

Solo travellers save the most in dorms, and shared kitchens cut food costs. Hostels are also the easiest place to meet people.

When a guesthouse wins

Two people sharing a private room often pay about the same as two dorm beds, and they get a door that locks and a quieter night.
