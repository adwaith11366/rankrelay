## What the article says

The draft is at travel-insurance-basics.html, the address cheap-flights-timing.html already links to. That address returns 404 in the crawl, so the campaign cannot start until a human publishes the article.

Its answer: yes, if losing the trip or paying a foreign hospital bill would hurt you; no, if everything is refundable, you are travelling close to home and you could cover an emergency from savings. It gives a two-question test (what do I lose if I can't go, and what happens if I get hurt abroad), says to check your credit card's benefits first, lists when to skip and when to buy, advises buying soon after the first nonrefundable payment, and says to compare quotes from two providers.

## Draft social posts

Both are for a human to post after the article is live. A human adds the page link when posting. No hashtags.

Post 1

Is travel insurance worth it on a budget trip? Ask two questions. What do you lose if you can't go? And what happens if you get hurt abroad? If both answers are "not much," you can reasonably go without. Our new guide walks through it.

Post 2

Before you buy a policy, read your credit card's benefits guide. Some cards cover medical costs, some cover only delays and baggage, and some cover nothing. Check four things: what it covers, whether you must book the whole trip on the card, the limits, and whether it pays first or second. Full guide on the site.
