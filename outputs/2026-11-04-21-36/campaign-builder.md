## What the article says

The campaign only repeats claims that are in the draft.

- The answer is "Yes, for most trips."
- Basic cover bundles four things: emergency medical care (and in some policies getting you home), trip cancellation or interruption, delayed or lost baggage, and theft of gear up to a limit per item.
- You can skip it only if the trip is short, every booking is refundable, your home health cover works in that country, and you carry nothing you couldn't afford to lose.
- Before buying, compare two or three policies on six points: medical limit, activities, pre-existing conditions, excess, cancellation reasons and claim rules.
- Buy when you book your flights, not the night before you leave.
- The article quotes no prices.

Is travel insurance worth it on a budget trip? For most trips, yes. One hospital bill or one missed flight can cost more than the rest of the trip. Basic cover bundles medical care, cancellation, baggage and gear theft. We wrote down when you can skip it, and it's a narrow list.
