Subject: Budget travel insurance guide, coming soon

Hello,

I work with Shoestring Atlas, a budget travel blog. We're finishing a guide called "Is travel insurance worth it on a budget trip?" and it will be published soon.

Here is what the draft contains. It answers the question first: yes for most trips, with a short list of cases where you can skip cover. It explains what basic cover pays for (emergency medical care, trip cancellation or interruption, delayed or lost baggage, and theft of gear up to a limit). It says to check a bank card's travel cover by reading the actual document. It lists six things to read before buying a cheap policy: the medical limit, activity exclusions, pre-existing conditions, the excess, cancellation reasons and claim rules. It says to buy when you book flights. It ends with three short answers on week-long trips, cancelled flights and buying after you've left home.

The guide quotes no prices.

If your page on budget travel resources could use a guide on this, I'll send you the link once it's live.

Thanks for reading,
The Shoestring Atlas team
