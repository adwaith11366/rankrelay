## What is being promoted

One article, drafted by the content writer for the URL travel-insurance-basics.html. That URL is a 404 today; cheap-flights-timing.html already links to it (lead-researcher.md). The article is a draft and is not published, so every promotion step below waits for a human to publish it. Day 1 is the publish day, whatever calendar date that turns out to be.

The article says the following, and the assets below stay inside it:

- The answer is yes in most cases. The test is whether one medical bill, a lost bag or a cancelled flight would wreck your budget.
- Medical cover is the part not to skimp on. Cut extras first (gadget cover, high luggage limits, sports add-ons).
- Check what you already have before buying: a credit or debit card, health insurance or a national health scheme, an employer or bank account package.
- A few countries and visa types ask for proof of cover. Check official entry requirements.
- Compare quotes on the same things: medical limit, cancellation limit, excess and exclusions.
- Buy early, because cancellation cover usually applies only to events after you buy.

The draft gives no prices, percentages, statistics or named insurers. None appear in the assets below.
