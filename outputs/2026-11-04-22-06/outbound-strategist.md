Status: draft for human approval. Nothing has been sent, posted or submitted.

## How this list was built

I ran four WebSearch queries on 2026-11-05 (budget travel resource pages, budget travel forums, budget travel insurance guides, weekly travel roundups). Every URL below is exactly as the search returned it. I did not open any of the pages, so "why it fits" rests on the titles and snippets the search gave me. Before anyone writes to a site, a person should open the page, find the right contact and confirm it is still maintained. Search volume, domain authority and contact details are n/a for every row.

The article these prospects are for is the unpublished draft in content-writer.md, "Do you need travel insurance on a budget trip?", planned for travel-insurance-basics.html. It is not live, so every angle below is "coming soon".

## Order of work

Rows 1, 2, 6, 7 and 8 take an email. Rows 3, 4 and 5 are forums, where an email is the wrong channel; they need a person who takes part in the community. Start with row 2 and row 6, because each is a single editor or small team who can say yes or no quickly.

## Outreach email draft

Draft, not sent. For human approval. Use for rows 1, 2, 6, 7 and 8 after a person has confirmed the contact and the page.

Subject: Budget travel insurance guide, coming soon

Hello,

I'm writing for Shoestring Atlas, a budget travel blog. We're about to publish a guide called "Do you need travel insurance on a budget trip?" and I thought it might suit your readers.

The guide gives a short test: if one medical bill, lost bag or cancelled flight would wreck your budget, buy cover. It lists what a policy usually covers, says which part not to skimp on (medical), and lists three places to check for cover you may already have (a credit or debit card, health insurance, an employer or bank package). It also covers when proof of insurance is required, how to compare quotes and why to buy early, and it ends with three short FAQs.

It isn't live yet. Once it is, I can send the link, so you can read it before deciding anything. If your page or newsletter ever needs a plain explainer on this question, we'd be glad to be considered.

Thank you for your time.
